Sales Analytic Report
🔍 Project Overview
This project presents a comprehensive Sales Analytics Dashboard designed to analyze sales performance across multiple dimensions including time, product hierarchy, sales channels, and sales representatives. The dashboard provides decision-makers with actionable insights to understand revenue trends, identify top-performing products and employees, and evaluate overall business performance.
🎯 Objectives
•	Monitor total revenue, average revenue, and order volume
•	Analyze yearly revenue trends
•	Identify top and bottom product groups
•	Evaluate performance by salesperson, manager, and sales channel
•	Support data-driven decision-making through interactive visuals
![Image](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/f2a00855-1df9-4af7-9292-da0b8717427e)

📈 Key KPIs
•	Total Revenue: $17.91M
•	Total Orders: 260K
•	Average Revenue per Order: $68.85
📊 Dashboard Insights
•	Revenue Trend by Year:
Shows revenue growth and decline patterns from 2019 to 2021, enabling historical performance comparison.
•	Revenue by Product Group:
Highlights top contributors such as Wheat Flour and Oil, helping prioritize high-performing products.
•	Revenue by Manager:
Compares managerial performance to assess leadership impact on sales.
•	Average Revenue by Channel:
Analyzes Retail, Distributor, and Online channels to understand customer purchasing behavior.
•	Salesperson Performance Table:
Provides detailed metrics including total revenue, average revenue, and order count for each salesperson.
🛠 Tools & Skills Used
•	Power BI / Excel
•	Data Modeling & DAX
•	Data Aggregation & KPIs
•	Interactive Visualizations
•	Business Performance Analysis
📌 Outcome
This dashboard enables stakeholders to quickly identify revenue drivers, assess employee performance, and optimize sales strategies based on reliable data insights.

 Revenue Analytic Report
🔍 Project Overview
This project focuses on advanced revenue analysis using time-intelligence metrics such as MTD, QTD, YTD, and Same Period Last Year (SPLY / SQLY). The dashboard is designed to track revenue performance over time, compare current results with historical benchmarks, and analyze revenue distribution across product categories, groups, and supervisors.
![Image](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/cde69f5f-52e1-4dbd-a6aa-b23fa236c977)

🎯 Objectives
•	Track revenue performance across different time frames
•	Compare current revenue with previous periods
•	Analyze revenue contribution by product category and product group
•	Evaluate supervisor-level performance
•	Identify trends and gaps to support strategic planning
📈 Key KPIs
•	Total Revenue: $17.91M
•	Average Revenue: $68.86
•	Order Count: 260K
📊 Dashboard Insights
•	SQLY & QTD Revenue by Quarter:
Provides quarter-over-quarter comparison to detect seasonal patterns and performance changes.
•	MTD Revenue Analysis:
Compares month-to-date revenue with the same month last year for performance tracking.
•	YTD Revenue by Product Group:
Identifies high and low-performing product groups such as Wheat Flour and Oil.
•	Revenue by Supervisor:
Compares supervisors’ revenue contribution using YTD and SQLY metrics.
•	Revenue by Product Category:
Visual breakdown of Food vs Drink categories across MTD, QTD, and YTD periods.
🛠 Tools & Skills Used
•	Power BI
•	DAX Time Intelligence (MTD, QTD, YTD, SPLY)
•	Data Modeling
•	Comparative Analysis
•	Interactive Reporting
📌 Outcome
This dashboard provides a clear understanding of revenue evolution over time, supports performance benchmarking, and helps management make informed, forward-looking decisions.
